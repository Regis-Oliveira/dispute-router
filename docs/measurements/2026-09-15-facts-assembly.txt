Runs are spaced 40s apart in the vector table. Back to back they are not
measurable: Voyage rate-limits this key and internal/llm/embed.go pauses a flat
25s per retry, up to three attempts, so a throttled assembly reads 25,5xx or
50,7xx ms and says nothing about the code. Both numbers appeared in the first
attempt at this table and neither is latency.

What the shape can and cannot buy. The fan-out removes the sum of every branch
except the slowest; it cannot touch the slowest one. Here the embedding call is
~400ms and the other three reads together are ~70ms, so ~70ms is the whole prize
and the floor is the call to Voyage. In lexical mode the slowest branch is the
evidence read (three S3 round trips, ~48ms) and the rest sum to ~8ms, which is
why the absolute saving is smaller there while the proportion is similar.

The honest reading: on loopback, against a Postgres and a LocalStack on the same
machine, this is a small win. The reason to keep it is that every one of those
branches is a network call in anything that is not this laptop, and the shape
stops the record's cost being the sum of them.
